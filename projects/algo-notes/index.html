"A markdown generated site for VCE Algorithmics notes"

2025

Introduction §

Back in year 12 I took all of my digital notes for VCE Algorithmics in Obsidian for VCE Algorithmics, a high school version of 'Algorithms and Analysis'.

When I got my job at CHES tutoring that same class, I discovered good ol' MkDocs. A documentation static site generator (SSG) that runs on markdown files. The same files that I wrote all my Obsidian notes in! As a learning exercise to try another SSG (other than 11ty) and to avoid printing 100's of pages of Obsidian notes for my students, I created algo.mcloughlan.com.

Site Demo §

A Note On SSGs §

I didn't lose any functionality at all going from Obsidian to MkDocs. I actually opened up the possibility for interactive demos, GIFs and public contribution.

For anyone with a wealth of knowledge they wish to make public, I would suggest giving this kind of thing a try. I even retained the LaTeX equations on the site.

Conclusion §

The creation of this website was the main source of value for me. I revised all my algo skills, used my first subdomain and learnt a lot about how to use markdown SSGs to store information online in a useful way. I plan to use something like MkDocs in future projects for documentation.
